# Mauritius implementation plan

**Implementation location:** Mauritius.  
**Purpose:** identify a practical local delivery network for mechanical, electrical, fabrication, procurement and testing work.  
**Status:** discovery only. No business below has accepted the project or been qualified to perform a complete EN125 electric conversion.

## 1. Delivery model

Do not assume one garage can deliver the whole conversion. Use a lead motorcycle workshop and specialist work packages:

1. Donor inspection, teardown, motorcycle geometry and reassembly.
2. Mechanical design, motor mounts, chain alignment and enclosure fabrication.
3. Motor/controller system selection and integration.
4. Battery pack engineering, BMS, charger and safety documentation.
5. Low-voltage wiring, instrumentation and diagnostics.
6. Bench and vehicle verification.
7. Separate regulatory/registration/insurance workstream.

The motorcycle workshop should coordinate the build and own the as-built record. Specialist suppliers must provide exact datasheets and written compatibility. A battery pack should come from a specialist pack manufacturer/integrator with traceable cells and documented tests; do not assume a solar-storage installer is automatically qualified for a high-discharge traction pack.

## 3. Supplier qualification status

All leads above are **UNQUALIFIED**. Public listings and marketing descriptions are not proof of EV conversion experience, high-voltage competence, welding quality or battery-pack capability.

For every potential partner, record:
- exact legal business name and current contact;
- relevant completed projects with photos/references;
- named technician and relevant training/experience;
- equipment and test capability;
- work package accepted and exclusions;
- drawings/specifications they require;
- quality inspection and sign-off process;
- price, lead time, warranty and rework terms;
- ability to provide as-built records.

## 4. External specialist sourcing

If a complete traction battery cannot be qualified locally, request a custom pack quotation from a specialist supplier such as Batt-Tech-SA (Durban, South Africa). It publicly advertises 48–96 V motorcycle packs, LFP/NMC options, matched BMS/chargers, motors/controllers and integration support. This is a supplier lead, not an endorsement or approved component selection.

Supplier page: https://batt-tech-sa.co.za/conversions/

Request a quotation only after the EN125 pack envelope, voltage, continuous/peak battery current, energy, chemistry, charging and connector requirements have been calculated.

## 5. Mauritius-specific sourcing approach

- Keep the donor motorcycle and mechanical fabrication local.
- Source ordinary steel, machining, fasteners and motorcycle consumables locally where specifications can be met.
- Source traction motor/controller and battery from suppliers that can provide exact manufacturer documentation and compatibility support.
- Compare landed cost, freight, dangerous-goods handling, customs, VAT/duty, warranty returns and spare-part lead times before choosing a foreign supplier.
- Prefer a complete matched motor/controller/battery/BMS/charger proposal over unrelated low-cost marketplace parts.
- Do not buy a generic 72 V scooter battery for a motorcycle traction application without verifying discharge current, BMS, cell provenance, enclosure and controller compatibility.

## 6. First qualification action

Send the donor photographs and a one-page project brief to Revival Customs and MotoQuest. Ask for a paid or unpaid initial inspection only after confirming what they can actually assess. Ask Integrity Metal Works or MetalWays to quote fabrication only after a dimensioned mount/enclosure drawing exists.
