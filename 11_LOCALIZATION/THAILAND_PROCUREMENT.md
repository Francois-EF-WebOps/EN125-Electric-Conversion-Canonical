## Contact-first procurement process

1. Send the same written request-for-information to at least two candidate suppliers.
2. Ask for exact manufacturer/model, datasheet, certificate/test report, stock, lead time, quote validity, warranty, returns and service path.
3. For battery suppliers, request cell provenance, pack configuration, BMS make/model, pack-level safety evidence and charger compatibility before discussing price.
4. For fabricators, request evidence of relevant material/process capability and willingness to work to reviewed drawings.
5. Record responses in the BOM and supplier register. No response means OPEN, not approved.

## External path

For traction motor/controller/charger and battery packs, start from manufacturers and authorised distributors rather than generic marketplace listings. Require exact model/revision; datasheet with continuous and peak ratings and thermal conditions; interface/pinout and configuration software documentation; pack test and transport evidence as applicable; written compatibility statement; serial/batch traceability; export/import, dangerous-goods shipping, warranty and repair pathway.

The design should keep interfaces sufficiently documented to permit alternate manufacturers, but substitutions require a fresh compatibility review. No “drop-in” equivalence is assumed.
