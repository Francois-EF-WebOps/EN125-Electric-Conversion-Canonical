# Thailand regulatory research — Suzuki EN125 motorcycle conversion

**Checked:** 2026-11-21  
**Status:** OPEN — written confirmation required for the exact motorcycle category and intended use. This is research, not legal advice.

## Critical scope distinction

Thailand has different legal routes depending on whether a vehicle is registered under the Motor Vehicle Act or the Land Transport Act. The Royal Gazette announcement dated 20 January 2026 found in this research is explicitly framed for vehicles used in passenger/goods transport under the Land Transport Act. It must not be treated as automatic approval for a privately registered Suzuki EN125 motorcycle.

## Sources found

1. **Royal Gazette / DLT announcement, 20 January 2026:** “Criteria, procedures and conditions for modifying a vehicle to be propelled by an electric motor under the Land Transport Act B.E. 2568.” The announcement states an effective date of 1 January 2026 and includes engineering requirements for electrical protection, emergency shutdown, motor mounting, battery/BMS, charging, documentation and qualified engineering certification. Source: https://ratchakitcha.soc.go.th/documents/101586.pdf
2. **DLT draft regulation under the Motor Vehicle Act:** DLT page dated 16 December 2025 shows a draft regulation for registration/tax handling of vehicles converted to electric propulsion and a public-comment period of 16–23 December 2025. This is evidence of a draft consultation, not proof that the final regulation was enacted. Source: https://legal.dlt.go.th/gen2/article/875
3. **Earlier DLT draft under the Motor Vehicle Act:** DLT page dated 7 July 2025. Source: https://legal.dlt.go.th/gen2/article/855
4. **TISI TIS 3630-2566:** conversion-kit standard for electric motorcycles; listed as a general/voluntary standard, effective 9 November 2023. Source: https://a.tisi.go.th/t/?n=7463
5. **TISI TIS 3712-2566:** lithium-ion battery system test specifications and safety requirements for electrically propelled mopeds/motorcycles; listed as a general standard, effective 6 July 2024. Source: https://a.tisi.go.th/t/?n=7598
6. **TISI มอก.เอส 222-2565:** service standard for electric motorcycle conversion; its scope does not itself cover all testing, structural certification or permit submission. Source: https://www.tisi.go.th/data/tis_s/s222-2565.pdf

## Stop condition

Do not remove the original engine, buy traction components, fabricate mounts, energise a pack or operate a modified vehicle until the exact Motor Vehicle Act route is confirmed in writing by DLT and the technical design is reviewed by appropriately licensed professionals.
