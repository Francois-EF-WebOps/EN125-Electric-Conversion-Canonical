# Verification and validation plan

## Purpose
Define how requirements will be demonstrated. This plan does not authorise live electrical work or vehicle operation.

## Stages

### V0 — document and configuration review
Verify donor identity, manuals, ratings and baseline records; confirm each selected part has an exact model/revision and datasheet; resolve jurisdiction and insurance gates. Output: reviewed configuration index and open-issue list.

### V1 — non-energised mechanical inspection
Review drawings, clearances, fastener design, material records and mount calculations; inspect battery enclosure/retention design and service access; review mass properties, axle loads, brakes, tyres and suspension. Output: signed mechanical design review. No road test.

### V2 — electrical design review
Review single-line diagram, conductor/protection calculations, isolation, shutdown, charging, grounding and control interfaces; review pack/BMS/charger compatibility and fault behaviour. Output: signed electrical review. No live traction testing.

### V3 — controlled bench verification
Only after qualified approval, using an authorised facility, calibrated equipment, approved procedures and defined emergency controls: component compatibility; protection/interlock behaviour; sensor plausibility and fault handling; controller/motor behaviour within documented limits; charger/BMS coordination. Record configuration, instruments, calibration, conditions, raw data, deviations and aborts.

### V4 — vehicle-level controlled verification
Only after V0–V3 pass and legal authorisation is documented: static checks and weight distribution; low-speed closed-area tests; braking, handling, thermal and range duty-cycle tests; environmental checks where specified. No public-road use until statutory and insurance requirements are satisfied.

### V5 — release and lifecycle
Final as-built drawings and BOM; operating, charging, maintenance and emergency manuals; reviewer/authority records; spare-parts and end-of-life pathway; release decision with explicit limitations.

## Test record minimum
Test ID, linked requirement, build/configuration ID, procedure revision, reviewer, date/location, instrument/model/serial/calibration, ambient conditions, expected result, measured result, pass/fail, deviations, corrective action, raw evidence link and sign-off.

## Acceptance rule
A requirement is PASS only when its defined acceptance criterion is met and objective evidence is attached. A successful single run does not prove durability, reliability, legal compliance or safe operation under untested conditions.
