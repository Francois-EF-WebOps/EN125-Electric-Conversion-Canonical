# Engineering claims and evidence boundary

This register controls what may be claimed publicly or on a CV.

| Claim | Permitted wording now | Evidence needed for stronger wording |
|---|---|---|
| Electric conversion | Developing an engineering dossier for a proposed EN125 electric conversion. | Build records, photographs, configuration list and inspection |
| Mechanical design | Documenting frame, mounting, mass and drivetrain design requirements. | Measured CAD, calculations and competent review |
| Electrical design | Developing traction/auxiliary architecture and protection requirements. | Complete schematics, component datasheets, calculations and review |
| Battery/BMS | Defining battery safety and evidence requirements. | Manufacturer test evidence, pack design review and controlled validation |
| Embedded control | Specifying control states, interlocks and fault behaviour. | Implemented source code, hardware target, test logs and review |
| Procurement | Building a traceable supplier and alternate-source process. | Written quotes, supplier qualification and actual purchase records |
| Verification | Creating staged verification and acceptance plans. | Approved procedures, instrument records, raw data and sign-offs |
| Road legality | No claim of approval or road legality. | Written determination and completed authority process for exact vehicle |
| CUDA/GPU | Original CUDA FIR benchmark scaffold and CPU reference are present; GPU execution and profiler evidence are pending. | Real NVIDIA GPU run, numerical validation, reproducible benchmark logs and profiling report |

## Evidence rules
- Separate measured facts, manufacturer specifications, calculations, assumptions and opinions.
- Preserve source URL, retrieval date, exact model/revision, page/section and local copy/hash where legally permitted.
- Never turn a generic supplier listing into proof of component suitability.
- Never turn a proposed design into a tested design.
- Never turn an authority's rule for one vehicle category into approval for another.
- Keep private identifiers, registration details, addresses and personal contact data out of public commits.
- For third-party code, record exact source URL, commit/tag, file path, licence, modifications and required notices before copying it.
