# Project brief — Suzuki EN125 electric conversion

## Objective
Develop a complete, reviewable engineering dossier for a proposed electric conversion of a Suzuki EN125 reference motorcycle. The immediate deliverable is a defensible engineering portfolio, not a claim that a vehicle has been converted.

## Engineering problem
Replace the original internal-combustion powertrain with electric traction while preserving or demonstrating acceptable vehicle-level performance, structural integrity, braking, handling, electrical safety, serviceability, legal admissibility and lifecycle economics. The design cannot be closed until the exact donor variant and intended duty cycle are known.

## Work packages
1. Requirements and baseline: exact donor year/variant, measured dimensions, mass, ratings, use case and legal category.
2. Architecture: system boundary, power domains, control interfaces, interlocks and fault responses.
3. Mechanical: frame, mounting, mass distribution, drivetrain, vibration, weather exposure and service access.
4. Electrical: traction/auxiliary domains, protection, isolation, harness, instrumentation and shutdown.
5. Battery: cells/pack, BMS, charger, enclosure, thermal management, retention and end-of-life evidence.
6. Safety: hazard register with controls, owners and verification evidence.
7. Procurement: qualified suppliers, manufacturer documentation, second sources and landed-cost records.
8. Verification: staged inspections, bench tests, controlled tests, acceptance criteria and sign-off.
9. Economics: petrol baseline, conversion and factory-built EV comparison on equivalent use.
10. Portfolio: traceability, calculations, design decisions, test records and limitations.

## Design freeze gates
- G0 identity: exact donor variant and legal category confirmed.
- G1 requirements: measurable use case and baseline recorded.
- G2 concept: architecture and trade study reviewed.
- G3 detailed design: calculations, component datasheets, interfaces and hazard controls complete.
- G4 procurement release: written professional review and legal route confirmed.
- G5 build release: controlled work package, qualified personnel, tools and inspection plan approved.
- G6 test release: facility, instrumentation, safety controls and abort criteria approved.
- G7 operational release: authority, registration, insurance and acceptance evidence complete.

A gate cannot pass because a document exists. It passes only when its evidence and reviewer are recorded.
