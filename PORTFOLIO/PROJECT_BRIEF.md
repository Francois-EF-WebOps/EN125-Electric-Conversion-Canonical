# Project brief — Suzuki EN125 electric conversion

## Objective
Develop and execute an evidence-led engineering path to convert a Suzuki EN125 from petrol to battery-electric propulsion in Mauritius. The immediate work is a reviewable engineering dossier and a buildable design; no physical conversion is claimed until measured and tested evidence exists.

## Engineering problem
Replace the original internal-combustion powertrain with electric traction while preserving acceptable vehicle-level performance, structural integrity, braking, handling, electrical safety, serviceability and maintainability. Statutory road-use approval and insurance are tracked separately from technical feasibility and prototype development.

## Preferred baseline concept
A mid-drive traction motor mounted in the original engine bay, driving the existing rear wheel through a calculated single chain reduction. This is supported by documented conversions of a Suzuki GSF600 Bandit and Honda NSR150. It remains a concept pending EN125-specific measurements, road-load sizing, packaging and review. A hub motor or retained gearbox may be compared if the baseline fails a defined requirement; neither should create parallel scope without evidence.

## Work packages
1. Requirements and baseline: exact donor year/variant, measured dimensions, mass, ratings, use case and intended performance.
2. Evidence and sizing: documented conversion cases, road-load model, motor/ratio selection and battery energy/power calculations.
3. Architecture: system boundary, power domains, control interfaces, interlocks and fault responses.
4. Mechanical: frame, mounting, mass distribution, drivetrain, vibration, weather exposure and service access.
5. Electrical: traction/auxiliary domains, protection, isolation, harness, instrumentation and shutdown.
6. Battery: cells/pack, BMS, charger, enclosure, thermal management, retention and end-of-life evidence.
7. Safety: hazard register with controls, owners and verification evidence.
8. Mauritius delivery: qualified workshop, fabricator, electrical specialist and external component/battery supplier as needed.
9. Procurement: exact models, datasheets, compatibility, supplier evidence, second sources and landed-cost records.
10. Verification: staged inspections, bench tests, controlled tests, acceptance criteria and sign-off.
11. Economics: petrol baseline, conversion and factory-built EV comparison on equivalent use.
12. Portfolio: traceability, calculations, design decisions, test records and limitations.

## Engineering gates
- G0 identity: exact donor variant and condition confirmed.
- G1 requirements: measurable use case and baseline recorded.
- G2 concept: architecture and trade study reviewed.
- G3 detailed design: calculations, component datasheets, interfaces and hazard controls complete.
- G4 procurement/build design release: technical compatibility and competent mechanical/electrical review complete.
- G5 fabrication/assembly: controlled work package, qualified personnel, tools and inspection plan approved.
- G6 test release: facility, instrumentation, safety controls and abort criteria approved.
- G7 road-use release: statutory approval, registration, insurance and acceptance evidence complete.

G0–G6 address engineering and controlled prototype work. G7 is a separate road-use gate. A gate cannot pass because a document exists; it passes only when evidence and reviewer are recorded.
