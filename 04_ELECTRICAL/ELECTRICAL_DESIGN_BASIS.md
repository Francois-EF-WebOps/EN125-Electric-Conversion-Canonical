# Electrical design basis

**Status:** requirements and design questions only. No wiring instructions or live-work procedure is provided.

## Design deliverables
- Functional single-line diagram with voltage domains and isolation boundaries.
- Power budget for traction and every auxiliary load.
- Motor/controller compatibility matrix.
- Conductor, connector and protection coordination calculations.
- Overcurrent protection and fault-current assumptions.
- Emergency shutdown and service-isolation concept.
- Insulation, leakage, ingress, vibration and abrasion protection requirements.
- DC/DC and low-voltage load budget.
- EMC/noise assessment for control and instrumentation.
- Charger/vehicle interface and charge interlock.
- Electrical design review by an appropriately licensed professional.

## Required evidence per component
Manufacturer, exact model/revision, operating voltage range, continuous and peak current, thermal derating, environmental rating, connector/pinout, protection requirements, installation constraints, warranty, compliance/test reports and traceable datasheet revision.

## Control and power separation
Signal wiring must be treated separately from traction conductors. Address noise coupling, grounding/reference strategy, fault propagation and safe behaviour when a sensor, communication link or supply fails.

## Safety boundary
Do not probe, connect, disconnect, energise, repair or test a traction battery or high-energy DC system based on this document. Such work requires qualified personnel, appropriate facilities, approved procedures and verified isolation.
