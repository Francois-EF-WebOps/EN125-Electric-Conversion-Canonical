# Project scope and limits

## Included
- Engineering requirements and traceability.
- EN125 donor baseline and measurement plan.
- Concept-level electric powertrain and vehicle architecture.
- Mechanical, electrical, battery, embedded-control and safety design bases.
- Supplier qualification and resilient procurement.
- Thailand-specific regulatory research, separated by vehicle category.
- Verification planning, cost modelling and professional portfolio presentation.

## Excluded until separately approved
- Instructions for live high-voltage assembly or energisation.
- Unsupervised traction-battery construction, repair or modification.
- Public-road testing or passenger/cargo operation.
- Claims of legal approval, certification, roadworthiness, range, performance or savings.
- Final selection of motor, controller, battery, charger, fuse, contactor or cable without system calculations and professional review.

## Required inputs
1. Exact Suzuki EN125 year, variant, frame/engine documentation and condition.
2. Intended operating profile: route, distance, gradient, speed, payload, duty cycle and charging access.
3. Baseline mass, axle loads, wheel/tyre/brake dimensions and frame measurements.
4. Local jurisdiction and intended use classification.
5. Competent mechanical/electrical reviewers and an authorised test pathway.

## Decision principle
Safety, legal admissibility and maintainability are hard constraints. Cost, range, performance and convenience are optimised only after those constraints are satisfied.
