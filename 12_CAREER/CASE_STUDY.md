# Portfolio case study — engineering a motorcycle electrification proposal

## Summary
An evidence-led systems-engineering study to define requirements, architecture, safety case, procurement strategy and verification pathway for a proposed Suzuki EN125 electric conversion.

## Capabilities demonstrated
Requirements engineering and traceability; systems architecture and interface definition; mechanical/electrical integration analysis; embedded-control state-machine specification; battery/BMS and charging requirements; hazard analysis and risk-based gates; supplier qualification and second-source planning; regulatory research with jurisdiction/category boundaries; verification planning and evidence management; lifecycle cost modelling; Git-based change control and reproducibility.

## What exists now
Project brief, scope boundary, requirements register, baseline measurement template, conceptual architecture, mechanical/electrical/battery design bases, hazard register, procurement framework, verification plan, Thailand research note, TCO model and this case study.

## What is not claimed
No donor-specific measurement set, selected component configuration, detailed CAD package, signed calculations, qualified BOM, physical build, bench test, road test, registration approval or certification.
