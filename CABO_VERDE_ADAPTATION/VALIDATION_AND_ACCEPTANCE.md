# Validation and acceptance checklist

This checklist is for reviewing the research package and any future pilot. It is not a vehicle certification.

## A. Repository integrity

- [ ] Canonical branch remains unchanged by this adaptation.
- [ ] All new material is isolated under `CABO_VERDE_ADAPTATION/`.
- [ ] Baseline commit and adaptation branch are stated.
- [ ] Every internal Markdown link resolves to a file in this folder.
- [ ] CSV opens correctly and columns remain consistent.
- [ ] No document describes planned work as completed work.
- [ ] Every source URL is stable enough to identify the referenced institution/instrument.
- [ ] Exact law text and current amendments are retrieved before legal conclusions.

## B. Evidence quality

- [ ] Each factual claim has a claim ID or clear citation.
- [ ] Source type, date checked, geography, and limitations are recorded.
- [ ] Government targets are distinguished from achieved outcomes.
- [ ] National infrastructure counts are distinguished from usable local infrastructure.
- [ ] Estimates and scenario assumptions are visibly labelled.
- [ ] No unsupported savings, emissions, range, safety, market, or job-creation claims remain.
- [ ] Open questions are marked OPEN, not silently treated as facts.

## C. Use-case definition

- [ ] One island/municipality and one bounded recurring task are identified.
- [ ] Actual route, payload, stops, schedule, weather, and terrain are recorded.
- [ ] Demand is evidenced by a real operator/customer or a documented baseline.
- [ ] Existing transport and practical alternatives are compared.
- [ ] Charging access and service/parts support are verified for the chosen location.

## D. Legal and technical gates

- [ ] Current legal requirements for the exact modified vehicle are confirmed in writing.
- [ ] Required registration, inspection, homologation, or approval steps are complete.
- [ ] Insurance covers the exact configuration and intended use in writing.
- [ ] Competent engineering review is documented.
- [ ] Battery, charging, electrical, mechanical, braking, and emergency risks are reviewed.
- [ ] Controlled test plan, instruments, acceptance thresholds, and abort conditions are approved.
- [ ] No public-road or commercial test occurs before required approvals.

## E. Economic and livelihood validation

- [ ] Costs use local quotes or labelled scenario assumptions.
- [ ] Petrol, factory-built EV, conversion, and relevant non-motorised/shared alternatives are compared on a like-for-like workload.
- [ ] Labour, downtime, compliance, battery replacement, and end-of-life are included.
- [ ] Cost per completed job and availability are calculated from recorded data.
- [ ] Sensitivity analysis shows which assumptions change the decision.
- [ ] Paid work is linked to actual demand, roles, compensation, and a credible workload.

## F. Release decision

Select exactly one:
- [ ] **HOLD:** key evidence or approvals are missing.
- [ ] **STOP:** legal, safety, demand, or economic case fails.
- [ ] **REDESIGN:** a defined gap may be resolved and re-reviewed.
- [ ] **PROCEED TO NEXT STAGE:** all gates for that stage passed; this does not imply permission for a later stage.

## Minimum public claim standard

A public statement must state what was tested, where, when, by whom (role/qualification where relevant), under what conditions, with what sample size, and what was not established. Do not use “certified”, “approved”, “road legal”, “safe”, “sustainable”, or “profitable” unless the exact evidence and authority support that precise claim.
