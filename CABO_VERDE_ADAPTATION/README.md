# Cabo Verde adaptation — review package

**Status:** research and planning draft; not an engineering approval, roadworthiness certificate, legal opinion, investment recommendation, or proof that a conversion is viable.

## Why this folder exists

This folder explores whether the EN125 electric-conversion learning framework could support a tightly scoped, evidence-led transport and livelihood study in Cabo Verde. It is deliberately separate from the canonical project material. It does not redefine the frozen reference, certify the donor motorcycle, or imply that converting an EN125 is legal in Cabo Verde.

## Decision question

Can a specific, legally admissible electric two-wheeler or conversion provide a safe, maintainable, and economically defensible service for a defined recurring local trip or delivery task—compared with the actual alternatives?

The study must be willing to conclude **do not convert**, **buy a factory-built EV**, **use an existing petrol vehicle**, or **stop pending evidence**.

## Non-negotiable rules

- No road-use testing until qualified local review confirms the exact vehicle configuration can legally be used and insured.
- No battery, motor, controller, braking, frame, wiring, or charging instructions are a substitute for a qualified engineer or competent technician.
- No unsupported claims that conversions are cheaper, safer, cleaner, or create jobs.
- No guessed prices, routes, charging access, range, payload, demand, or legal permissions.
- Every material factual claim must have a source, date checked, geographic scope, limitations, and evidence status.
- Preserve the canonical branch and existing artifacts. Review this adaptation as a separate change set.

## Evidence status vocabulary

- **VERIFIED:** primary or authoritative source checked; scope and date recorded.
- **SUPPORTED:** credible evidence exists but a material limitation remains.
- **OPEN:** not yet verified; do not rely on it for a go decision.
- **CONTRADICTED:** available evidence conflicts with the claim.
- **NOT APPLICABLE:** claim does not apply to the selected use case, with reason recorded.
