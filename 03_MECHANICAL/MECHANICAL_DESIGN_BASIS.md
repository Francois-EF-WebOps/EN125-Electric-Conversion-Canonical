# Mechanical design basis

## Objective
Demonstrate that the proposed motor, battery, mounts and ancillary equipment can be packaged without compromising structural integrity, braking, steering, handling, ground clearance, service access or permitted vehicle mass.

## Required baseline
Populate `01_REQUIREMENTS/BASELINE_MEASUREMENTS.csv` before drawing a final layout. Record donor model year/variant and condition. Photograph reference points with a scale; retain private vehicle identifiers outside the public repository.

## Analyses required
- Frame geometry and condition, including corrosion, cracks, prior repairs and material uncertainty.
- Original engine mass, centre-of-gravity estimate, mount locations and drive interface.
- Proposed motor mass, peak/continuous torque, speed and mounting reactions.
- Battery mass, envelope, centre of gravity and retention load cases.
- Front/rear axle load change and gross-mass compliance.
- Braking and tyre capability under changed mass distribution.
- Suspension travel, chain/belt alignment, ground clearance and rider ergonomics.
- Fatigue, vibration, fastener retention, corrosion and water ingress.
- Crash/impact exposure, guarding and service removal path.

## Required engineering artefacts
1. Dimensioned donor baseline drawing.
2. Packaging envelope and mass-property model.
3. Motor-mount drawing and load-path calculations.
4. Battery enclosure/retention drawing and load calculations.
5. Fastener and material specification with traceable standards.
6. Brake/tyre/suspension impact assessment.
7. Independent mechanical review and signed disposition.

## Prohibited shortcuts
Do not infer frame capacity from appearance, use unverified generic EN125 dimensions, weld or drill the frame based on a concept sketch, or assume original engine mounts can carry a different motor's peak torque. No final mount or battery location is approved by this document.
