# KIT.md — EN125 Conversion Requirements and Procurement Review

**Purpose:** turn the canonical educational material into a safe planning and professional-review package. This kit is not a step-by-step high-voltage build instruction.

## Read first
- `README.md`
- `LICENSE_CONTEXT.md`
- all existing safety, legal and compatibility notes
- applicable local vehicle, roadworthiness, registration and electrical rules

## Requirements artefacts
Before sourcing parts, document:
- exact motorcycle model/year and measured baseline;
- intended range, speed, load, duty cycle and charging conditions;
- motor/controller/battery compatibility evidence;
- battery chemistry, BMS, fusing, isolation, enclosure and thermal requirements;
- frame/brake/suspension/tyre load implications;
- environmental exposure, serviceability and end-of-life plan;
- legal approval, inspection and insurance requirements;
- qualified reviewer and acceptance criteria.

## Procurement pack
Create a BOM only after specifications and a qualified review. For each item record manufacturer/model, ratings, compatibility source, quantity, supplier/date, price, shipping, warranty, certification, alternative and total cost. Never substitute an unverified battery, charger, controller or protective device based only on appearance or price.

## Safe procedure
1. Inventory and preserve the existing canonical documents.
2. Gather model-specific measurements and official component datasheets.
3. Map every requirement to evidence and a reviewer.
4. Request a written compatibility and safety review from a qualified EV/electrical professional.
5. Verify legal conversion and registration requirements before purchase.
6. Do not energise, modify or test traction batteries without qualified supervision and appropriate isolation, PPE and facilities.
7. Record test plans and professional sign-off before any road use.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Every critical component has a compatibility source.
- [ ] Electrical, thermal, mechanical and road-use risks have named controls.
- [ ] Local legal status is checked and dated.
- [ ] BOM costs and alternatives are evidenced.
- [ ] Unverified assumptions are visible.
- [ ] No unsafe build or road-use claim is presented as validated.

**Stop:** damaged/swollen batteries, exposed conductors, uncertain ratings, missing isolation/protection or absent legal approval require professional assessment, not improvisation.
